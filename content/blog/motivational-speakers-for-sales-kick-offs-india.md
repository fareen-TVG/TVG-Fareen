A sales kick-off sets the tone for the whole year. Targets are new, teams are meeting in one room, and leaders want everyone to leave with energy and a plan. The right keynote speaker turns that moment into something people still talk about in June.

Most companies hold their sales kick-off between January and March, which means the best speakers get booked early. If you are planning yours now, here are 10 speakers from our roster who work brilliantly at sales conferences, grouped by the message you want your team to take home.

## Speakers who build a winning team mindset

Sales is a team sport. These sporting greats bring lessons from the dressing room that translate straight to the sales floor.

[[speaker:anil-kumble]]

The former India captain is known for his integrity, resilience and calm under pressure. His talks on leadership, handling pressure and team building land especially well with corporate audiences and first-time managers.

[[speaker:jonty-rhodes]]

One of cricket's greatest fielders, Jonty redefined what commitment looks like on the field. Now an international coach, he speaks on excellence, teamwork, discipline and sustained performance. Expect energy: his sessions are a natural fit for an opening keynote.

[[speaker:brett-lee]]

Few people know more about performing under pressure than one of the fastest bowlers the game has seen. Brett speaks on teamwork, managing pressure and maintaining peak performance, and his charisma makes him a memorable choice for international teams.

[[speaker:pullela-gopichand]]

India's most influential badminton coach has built world-class athletes from scratch. His sessions on discipline, preparation and building winning systems are ideal if your kick-off theme is about creating a high-performance sales culture.

## Speakers who know how to sell

Want your team to hear from people who have built brands customers love? These founders and marketers speak the language of growth.

[[speaker:aman-gupta]]

The co-founder and CMO of boAt is one of India's best-known entrepreneurs and a Shark Tank India favourite. He speaks on consumer insight, disruptive marketing and scaling a business, with an energetic style that suits younger sales teams.

[[speaker:harish-bhat]]

A Director at the Tata Group, bestselling author and celebrated marketer, Harish brings storytelling and consumer insight to the stage. His sessions on branding and building trusted businesses are perfect for teams that sell on relationships.

[[speaker:nitin-saluja]]

The founder of Chaayos reimagined India's tea culture through customer obsession and constant experimentation. His practical lessons on scaling consumer brands work well for retail, FMCG and D2C sales teams.

## Speakers who lead from the top

For leadership offsites or senior sales meetings, these speakers bring boardroom experience and frameworks your managers can use the next day.

[[speaker:shiv-shivakumar]]

Having led major organisations including PepsiCo and Aditya Birla Group, Shiv shares clear frameworks on leadership, strategy, career growth and navigating disruption.

[[speaker:radhika-gupta]]

The MD and CEO of Edelweiss Mutual Fund is known for her clarity and authenticity. She speaks on leadership, overcoming self-doubt and career resilience, which makes her a strong choice for teams coming off a tough year.

## A speaker for balance and focus

[[speaker:gaur-gopal-das]]

Big targets bring big pressure. Gaur Gopal Das connects spiritual wisdom with everyday professional challenges, speaking on purpose, relationships and emotional balance. He is a popular closing speaker who sends teams home calm and focused.

## How to choose the right sales kick-off speaker

- **Start with your theme.** Decide the one message you want people to remember, such as teamwork, resilience or customer focus, then choose a speaker known for it.
- **Think about your audience.** Young field teams often respond to founders and creators. Senior leaders may prefer boardroom experience or a sporting captain.
- **Plan the slot.** An opening keynote needs energy. A closing session works best with a speaker who inspires reflection.
- **Book early.** January to March is peak season for sales kick-offs, so popular speakers fill their calendars months ahead.
