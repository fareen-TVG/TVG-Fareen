International Women's Day falls on Monday, 8 March 2027. For many companies, colleges and associations, it is one of the most important events of the year: a chance to celebrate the women in your organisation and spark real conversations about leadership, confidence and inclusion.

A powerful keynote speaker can make the day meaningful rather than just ceremonial. Below are 12 inspiring women speakers from our roster, grouped by the kind of story your audience will connect with.

## Business leaders and founders

These women built and lead some of India's most talked-about companies. They are a great fit for corporate audiences, leadership programmes and women's networks.

[[speaker:radhika-gupta]]

The MD and CEO of Edelweiss Mutual Fund is a bestselling author known for her clarity and authenticity. She speaks on leadership, overcoming self-doubt, career resilience and building confidence.

[[speaker:vineeta-singh]]

The co-founder and CEO of Sugar Cosmetics and a Shark Tank India judge, Vineeta is celebrated for her grit and bold decisions. She speaks on entrepreneurship, women in leadership and disciplined execution.

[[speaker:yamini-bhat]]

The co-founder and CEO of Vymo, an AI-driven sales productivity platform, speaks on digital transformation, scaling technology companies and women in leadership. A strong choice for tech and business audiences.

[[speaker:chetna-sinha]]

Founder of the Mann Deshi Foundation, Chetna has spent her career empowering rural women through financial inclusion and entrepreneurship. Her talks on grassroots leadership and women's empowerment suit CSR and purpose-led events.

## Champions who broke barriers in sport

Few stories inspire like those of athletes who reached the top against the odds.

[[speaker:mithali-raj]]

India's most accomplished women's cricketer and former national captain speaks on leadership, gender equality in sport and staying motivated through adversity.

[[speaker:sania-mirza]]

One of India's greatest tennis players, Sania speaks on confidence, overcoming barriers, mental strength and championing women's voices.

[[speaker:deepa-malik]]

India's first female Paralympic medallist, Deepa is a celebrated speaker on resilience, courage and redefining limits. Her story resonates deeply at inclusion-focused events.

[[speaker:saina-nehwal]]

The Olympic medallist and world badminton champion speaks on goal setting, handling setbacks and building a champion's mindset, and connects especially well with young audiences.

## Leaders in uniform and public service

[[speaker:dr-kiran-bedi]]

India's first female IPS officer shares lessons on courage, discipline, integrity and public service. Her dynamic presence inspires audiences to lead with conviction.

[[speaker:captain-yashika-tyagi]]

A trailblazing former Army officer, Captain Yashika speaks on women's empowerment, decision-making under pressure, resilience and breaking stereotypes.

[[speaker:major-vandana-sharma]]

A distinguished Army veteran and advocate for women in uniform, Major Vandana speaks on resilience, career transitions and leading with integrity.

## A voice for growth and inclusion

[[speaker:sudha-menon]]

A bestselling author, columnist and leadership coach, Sudha runs talks and workshops on women's empowerment, personal growth, mindfulness and inclusive leadership. Ideal if you want an interactive session rather than a keynote.

## Tips for planning your Women's Day event

- **Pick a theme early.** Leadership, financial independence, breaking barriers and wellbeing are all popular. Your theme will guide your choice of speaker.
- **Mix formats.** A keynote followed by a fireside chat or Q&A gives your audience time to engage.
- **Include everyone.** The best Women's Day events invite men and women to listen and take part.
- **Book now.** 8 March is one of the busiest days of the year for women speakers, and many are booked months in advance.
