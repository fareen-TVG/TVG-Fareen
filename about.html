About TalentVerse Global

We Don't Just
Book Speakers.
We Curate Moments.

For over a decade, TalentVerse Global has been the agency that the world's most ambitious organisations turn to when a speech must do more than inform — when it must move, inspire, and change things.

Our Story

Born from a
Belief in Ideas

TalentVerse Global was founded in 2013 by two event producers who were tired of the transactional nature of speaker booking. They believed there had to be a better way — one built on deep relationships with both clients and speakers.

Today we represent over 600 speakers across every discipline, serving clients from Mumbai to Manchester, Singapore to São Paulo. Our team of specialist consultants matches the right voice to every stage.
