Privacy policy

Last updated 1 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information TalentVerse Global (“we”) collects through this website, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Details you type into our forms: name, email, phone number, organisation, event details and, for speaker applications, your bio and links.
  • Basic usage data (pages visited, device and browser) if analytics is enabled on this site.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry or application and arrange bookings you ask us to.
  • To understand which pages are useful and improve the site.

We don’t sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Who processes it

Form submissions are delivered to our inbox by EmailJS. If analytics is enabled, Google Analytics processes usage data. When you book a speaker, we share the event details needed with that speaker and their team.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to handle your request and any resulting booking, then delete them unless we’re required to keep records.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing unlock@talentverseglobal.com.
