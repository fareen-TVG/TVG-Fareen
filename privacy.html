Privacy policy

Last updated 16 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data TalentVerse Global collects, why we collect it, who we share it with and the choices you have. It is written to meet India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

By using this website or sending us your details, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not submit your details.

1. Who we are

TalentVerse Global is a speaker bureau based in Mumbai, India (“TalentVerse Global”, “we”, “us”). We decide how your personal data is used, which makes us the Data Fiduciary under Indian law.

This policy covers talentverseglobal.com and any enquiry, booking or speaker application you make with us by form, email, phone or WhatsApp.

2. What we collect

When you enquire or book a speaker:

  • Your name, email address, phone number and organisation.
  • Event details: date, location, audience size, budget, theme and any speaker you have in mind.
  • Anything else you choose to tell us in a message, email, call or WhatsApp chat.
  • Billing and contract details if a booking goes ahead.

When you apply to join our speaker roster:

  • Your name, contact details, area of expertise, bio, website and profile links.
  • Your photo, fees and availability if you share them with us later.

When you visit the website:

  • Usage data such as the pages you visit, how you arrived, and your device, browser and approximate location. This is collected through cookies (see section 8).
  • Technical data such as your IP address, which our hosting provider records for security.

From other people: a speaker’s management team or a colleague may give us your details when they introduce you to us.

We do not ask for sensitive data such as health, religion, caste or financial account details through this website. Please do not send it to us.

3. Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and send you a speaker shortlist.
  • To check availability and fees with speakers, and to arrange contracts, briefings, travel and on-the-day support for a booking.
  • To assess speaker applications and, with your approval, show your profile on our website.
  • To send invoices and keep the records the law requires.
  • To understand how the website is used and improve it.
  • To protect the website and our business from fraud, spam and misuse.

We only use your data for these purposes, or for another purpose that you have agreed to or that the law allows.

4. Your consent

We process your data because you give it to us for a purpose, for example to get a shortlist, or because the law allows it. You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing unlock@talentverseglobal.com. Withdrawing it does not affect anything we did before, but we may not be able to complete a booking without the details it needs.

5. Marketing messages

We do not send newsletters or promotional messages at present. If we start, we will only send them to people who have agreed to receive them, and every message will tell you how to stop. You can opt out at any time by replying “unsubscribe” or emailing us.

6. Speaker profiles

If you are a speaker on our roster, we publish your name, photo, title, bio and topics to promote you. These details appear on your profile page and in our speaker listings, and we include them in the shortlists and proposals we send to event organisers.

You or your management can ask us to update or remove your profile at any time by emailing unlock@talentverseglobal.com, and we will make the change promptly.

7. Who we share it with

We never sell your personal data. We share it only when needed:

  • Speakers and their management: the event details needed to check availability and deliver a booking.
  • Clients: if you are a speaker, the profile and booking details a client needs to consider and book you.
  • Service providers who work for us under contract and only on our instructions: EmailJS (delivers website form submissions to our inbox), our email and hosting providers, Google Analytics (website statistics), Google Fonts (website fonts) and WhatsApp (if you message us there).
  • Travel and event partners: such as airlines and hotels, when we arrange a speaker’s travel.
  • Authorities: when the law, a court or a government body requires it, or to protect our rights or someone’s safety.

8. Cookies and analytics

Cookies are small files saved by your browser. We use Google Analytics cookies to count visits and see which pages are useful. These cookies do not tell us who you are. We will ask for your consent before setting them, and you can change your choice at any time.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use Google’s opt-out add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout. The website works without them.

9. Where your data is stored

Some of our service providers, including Google and EmailJS, store data on servers outside India. We only use providers that protect data to a standard at least as strong as this policy, and we follow any restrictions the Government of India places on transfers to particular countries.

10. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries and speaker applications: up to 36 months after our last contact with you, so we can help with future events.
  • Contracts, invoices and booking records: for as long as Indian tax and accounting laws require.
  • Website analytics: up to 14 months.

After that we delete your data or make it anonymous. If you ask us to delete it sooner, we will, unless the law requires us to keep it.

11. How we protect it

We use reasonable security measures to protect your data, including secure (HTTPS) connections, access limited to the people who need it, and password-protected accounts. No system is completely secure, but if a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

12. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you, how we use it and who we have shared it with.
  • Ask us to correct, complete or update it.
  • Ask us to delete it.
  • Withdraw your consent.
  • Raise a complaint with us and get a response.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights for you if you die or become unable to.

To use any of these rights, email unlock@talentverseglobal.com. We may ask you to confirm your identity first. We aim to respond within 30 days.

13. Children

This website is for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you think a child has sent us their details, please tell us and we will delete them.

14. Other websites

Our website links to other sites, such as speakers’ own websites and social media. We are not responsible for how those sites handle your data, so please read their privacy policies.

15. Questions and complaints

If you have a question or complaint about your personal data, contact our Grievance Officer:

Grievance Officer, TalentVerse Global
Mumbai, India

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the date at the top of this page and, for important changes, let you know by email or a notice on the website.

17. Governing law

This policy is governed by the laws of India. Any dispute about it will be handled by the courts in Mumbai.
