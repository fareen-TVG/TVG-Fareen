Navtej Sarna

Former Indian Ambassador to the United States · Award-winning Author

Navtej Sarna is a distinguished diplomat, acclaimed author, and former Indian Ambassador to the United States (2016–2018). A member of the Indian Foreign Service from 1980, he also served as India's High Commissioner to the United Kingdom, Ambassador to Israel, and Secretary in the Ministry of External Affairs.

He was India's longest-serving official spokesperson for the Ministry of External Affairs. An award-winning writer, his novel Crimson Spring received the Sahitya Akademi Award (2025) and the KLF Best Fiction Award (2022). Sarna has authored several acclaimed novels, non-fiction works and translations, and continues to write extensively on foreign policy, history and literature.

Speaking topics

  • India's foreign policy and its place in a changing world order
  • India–US and India–UK relations
  • Diplomacy, negotiation and public communication
  • History, literature and the craft of writing
