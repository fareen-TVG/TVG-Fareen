Former Indian Ambassador to the United States and Author
A distinguished diplomat, acclaimed author, and former Indian Ambassador to the United States (2016–2018). A member of the Indian Foreign Service from 1980, he also served as India's High Commissioner to the UK, Ambassador to Israel, and Secretary in the Ministry of External Affairs. He was India's longest-serving official spokesperson for the Ministry of External Affairs. An award-winning writer, his novel Crimson Spring received the Sahitya Akademi Award (2025) and the KLF Best Fiction Award (2022). Sarna has authored several acclaimed novels, non-fiction works, translations, and continues to write extensively on foreign policy, history, and literature.

About TalentVerse Global

We Don't Just
Book Speakers.
We Curate Moments.

For over a decade, TalentVerse Global has been the agency that the world's most ambitious organisations turn to when a speech must do more than inform — when it must move, inspire, and change things.

Our Story

Born from a
Belief in Ideas

TalentVerse Global was founded in 2013 by two event producers who were tired of the transactional nature of speaker booking. They believed there had to be a better way — one built on deep relationships with both clients and speakers.

Today we represent over 600 speakers across every discipline, serving clients from Mumbai to Manchester, Singapore to São Paulo. Our team of specialist consultants matches the right voice to every stage.

01

Curation Over Volume

We say no to speakers who don't meet our standard. Quality of impact always beats size of roster.

02

Deep Listening

We spend time understanding your audience before we ever suggest a name. The right speaker changes the room.

03

Full Service

From brief to backstage, we manage every detail so you can focus on your event.

04

Long-Term Partnership

85% of our clients return. We measure success in relationships, not transactions.
